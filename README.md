Project Overview & Tech Stack
This project is an immersive, gamified student ecosystem engineered to optimize academic productivity, physical well-being, peer collaboration, and campus navigation. Built with a high-performance FastAPI and MongoDB backend paired with a React web application and a Flutter cross-platform mobile app, the platform transforms everyday university life into an interactive, real-world utility and role-playing experience.

Core Hero Features
1)Offline-Capable Geolocation Navigation System: Features an advanced, offline-first campus routing mechanism using MAPTILER API. Designed for large university layouts, it guides students to classes and venues using device geolocation—ensuring seamless wayfinding even in areas with weak cellular or Wi-Fi coverage.

2)Smart Passage & Obstacle Routing: Dynamically recalculates campus walking paths around real-time road and passage blockages, construction zones, or crowded thoroughfares to ensure students always arrive on time.

3)Health & Activity Promotion Quests: Integrates dedicated physical wellness quests designed to actively promote health, fitness, and daily physical activity among students. Users complete movement-based challenges, earn health milestone badges, and maintain active fitness streaks.

4)VTOP Schedule Synchronization & Alerts: Automatically synchronizes institutional timetables and dispatches smart advance warnings (calculating precise walking times from residential zones or messes to academic blocks like AB-1).

5)Collaborative Study Groups & Skill Profiling: Connects peers for collaborative academic learning. User profiles highlight specific technical skills (e.g., Python, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity), enabling students to discover and team up with partners possessing complementary proficiencies.

6)Gamification & Global Leaderboard: Rewards productivity quests, health challenges, attendance, and study milestones with Experience Points (XP), streak counters, collectible achievement badges, and a competitive global ranking system.

7)Community Proof Gallery: Includes a media-sharing feed where students can upload and verify photo or video proof of completed quests in public or private view modes.
